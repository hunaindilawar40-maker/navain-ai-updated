A possible workflow

From a caller's question to your next step.

Your script and escalation rules determine the exact conversation. Here's the shape of a typical service request.

01 · Answer

Welcome the caller

Navain answers on your existing practice number, day or night, using the greeting selected for your practice.

02 · Understand

Ask the right questions

Collect the appointment request and contact details your team has approved.

03 · Decide

Book, move, or hand off

Use the connected calendar when appropriate, or follow your instructions for a person, urgent issue, or callback.

04 · Follow up

Send your team a summary

Get the caller's request and agreed next step by email and SMS after the call.

Navain is not an emergency-response service. For a life-threatening emergency, callers should contact local emergency services.

Common questions

Dental practices, answered.

Does Navain use our existing practice number?

Yes. Navain is set up to answer on your existing practice number; there's no new number or hardware to install.

Can it book or move a dental appointment?

Appointment booking and rescheduling are available when your calendar is connected. During setup, you choose the availability and rules the assistant should follow.

What if a caller has a clinical or urgent question?

Set the contact and fallback instructions during setup. Live transfer is available when configured; otherwise, Navain can capture the details for prompt follow-up. For a medical or dental emergency, callers should contact local emergency services; Navain is not an emergency-response service.

What will my team receive after a call?

Navain sends a call summary by email and SMS. The summary and any follow-up steps depend on the workflow you configure.
