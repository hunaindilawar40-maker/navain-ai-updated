About us

We missed those calls ourselves.

Navain AI isn't a lab experiment. It comes from the team behind Pontis Construction Inc.—a construction services company working across the US and Canada since 2010. Years of estimating, coordinating projects, and keeping clients informed showed us how much can depend on one answered call.

The problem we lived

When you're the one on the ladder, in the crawl space, or in a customer's kitchen, you cannot answer the phone. Voicemail doesn't answer it either — most callers hear the greeting and hang up, then call the next business on the list. And a full-time receptionist isn't practical for every crew, every location, or every budget.

That gap — between a phone that rings all day and hands that are always full — is where service businesses lose work. We felt it for years.

The receptionist we wanted

So we wrote down exactly what we needed: something that answers within two rings, at any hour, in our own voice. That books straight into our live calendar while the caller is still on the line. That sends us a summary by text and email after every call. That needs no hardware, no new phone number, and no contract — and that goes live within 24 hours.

Then we built it. Navain AI now answers for service businesses the way we always wished someone would answer for us.

“We know the feeling of seeing the phone ring when both hands are already occupied. Navain is our answer to that everyday problem: help the caller now, keep the business owner informed, and know when a person should step in.”

— The Navain AI team

The experience behind Navain

Pontis Construction Inc. has operated since 2010. Its services include construction cost estimating, material sourcing, pre-construction BIM, shop drawings, roofing, millwork, casework, and interior finishes for residential, commercial, and industrial work.

Pontis lists its US head office in Sacramento, California and its Canadian operational office in Richmond Hill, Ontario. Its website says the team has served more than 5,000 estimating and takeoff clients. Founder Jack David describes the company around innovation, transparency, collaboration, and long-term trust—the same practical values we are bringing to Navain.
