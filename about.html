About us

We missed those calls ourselves.

Navain AI isn't a lab experiment. It comes from the team behind Pontis Construction — a company that has been running service work across the US and Canada since 2010, and losing jobs to missed calls the entire time.

The problem we lived

When you're the one on the ladder, in the crawl space, or in a customer's kitchen, you cannot answer the phone. Voicemail doesn't answer it either — most callers hear the greeting and hang up, then call the next business on the list. And a full-time receptionist isn't practical for every crew, every location, or every budget.

That gap — between a phone that rings all day and hands that are always full — is where service businesses lose work. We felt it for years.

The receptionist we wanted

So we wrote down exactly what we needed: something that answers within two rings, at any hour, in our own voice. That books straight into our live calendar while the caller is still on the line. That sends us a summary by text and email after every call. That needs no hardware, no new phone number, and no contract — and that goes live within 24 hours.

Then we built it. Navain AI now answers for service businesses the way we always wished someone would answer for us.

How we work

  • Live on your existing number within 24 hours — no hardware, nothing to install
  • Custom agent voice and script for every business
  • Appointment booking with live calendar sync
  • Call summaries by email and SMS after every call
  • 24/7 coverage — nights, weekends, holidays
  • Month-to-month, no contracts — your number stays yours
