FAQ

Fair questions, straight answers

How fast can Navain AI go live?

Most businesses are live within 24 hours of signing up. We build your agent's voice and script, connect your calendar, and route your existing phone number — no new number, no hardware, and nothing to install.

Do I need special hardware or a new phone number?

No. Navain AI answers on your existing business number. There is no hardware to buy, no software to install, and no phone system to replace.

What does the AI receptionist actually do during a call?

It answers within two rings in your business's own voice, follows your custom script, answers questions about your services, and books appointments directly from your live calendar. When a booking isn't the right outcome, it takes a message. After every call you get a summary by text and email.

Will callers know they're talking to an AI?

Callers hear a natural, human-sounding voice using your custom greeting and script. The agent speaks as your front desk — most callers simply experience a receptionist who answers instantly and gets them booked.

What happens when the AI can't answer something?

It stays honest. The agent takes a message with the caller's details and the reason for the call, flags it for follow-up, and includes it in your call summary so nothing gets lost. On the Growth plan, the caller also gets an SMS follow-up after the call.

How much does Navain AI cost?

Essential is $699/month plus an $899 one-time setup. Growth is $999/month plus a $1,399 one-time setup, and adds SMS follow-up after every call, CRM integration with HubSpot and Go High Level, multi-location support, priority onboarding, and a monthly performance report. Enterprise is custom-priced with a dedicated account manager, custom integrations, white-label, and an SLA. Every plan is month-to-month.

Can I cancel anytime?

Yes. Navain AI is month-to-month with no contracts. If you cancel, your number stays yours — we simply stop answering it.
