FAQ

Fair questions, straight answers

How fast can Navain AI go live?

Most businesses are live within 24 hours of signing up. We build your agent's voice and script, connect your calendar, and route your existing phone number — no new number, no hardware, and nothing to install.

Do I need special hardware or a new phone number?

No. Navain AI answers on your existing business number. There is no hardware to buy, no software to install, and no phone system to replace.

What does the AI receptionist actually do during a call?

It answers within two rings in your business's own voice, follows your custom script, answers questions about your services, and books appointments directly from your live calendar. When a caller needs a person or a booking isn't the right outcome, it follows your configured handoff rules or takes a message for follow-up. After every call you get a summary by text and email.

Will callers know they're talking to an AI?

Navain is an AI receptionist, not a human employee. It uses a natural voice with your chosen greeting and script. During setup, you can shape how it introduces itself and when it hands a caller to a person.

What happens when the AI can't answer something?

It follows the escalation instructions agreed during setup. If a caller asks for a person, remains confused, raises an urgent issue, or the agent can't confidently help, Navain can route the call to your designated contact when live transfer is configured. If no one can take the call, it captures the details, flags the request for priority follow-up, and sends your team a summary. For a life-threatening emergency, callers should contact local emergency services; Navain is not an emergency-response service.

How much does Navain AI cost?

Essential is $699/month plus an $899 one-time setup. Growth is $999/month plus a $1,399 one-time setup, and adds SMS follow-up after every call, CRM integration with HubSpot and Go High Level, multi-location support, priority onboarding, and a monthly performance report. Enterprise is custom-priced with a dedicated account manager, custom integrations, white-label, and an SLA. Every plan is month-to-month.

Can I cancel anytime?

Yes. Navain AI is month-to-month with no contracts. If you cancel, your number stays yours — we simply stop answering it.

A real front desk knows when to pass it on

AI when it can.
Human when it should.

Routine calls stay quick and easy. If a caller needs a person, remains confused, raises an urgent issue, or the AI can't confidently help, the next step is part of your call plan — not a dead end.

  1. 01 · Recognize

    Spot the exception

    A caller asks for a human, stays confused after clarification, describes an urgent issue, or asks something outside the agent's confident answers.

  2. 02 · Route

    Follow your rules

    When live transfer is configured, connect to your chosen teammate or on-call contact. Otherwise, move to the message-and-follow-up path you set.

  3. 03 · Close the loop

    Keep the context

    If no one can take the call, capture the caller's details and reason, flag it for priority follow-up, and send your team a clear summary.

Emergency boundary: For immediate, life-threatening danger, the call flow should direct callers to local emergency services. Navain is not an emergency-response service.
