Terms of Service

The plain-language rules for using Navain AI. By using the service you agree to them.

Last updated: October 5, 2026

1. The service

Navain AI provides a 24/7 AI phone receptionist for service businesses. It answers calls on your existing number within two rings, in a custom voice and script, books appointments from your live calendar, and sends call summaries by email and SMS. The service goes live within 24 hours of a completed signup, with no hardware required.

2. Plans and payment

  • Essential — $699/month plus a $899 one-time setup.
  • Growth — $999/month plus a $1,399 one-time setup.
  • Enterprise — custom pricing, agreed in writing.

Setup fees are one-time. Monthly fees are billed in advance. All plans are month-to-month.

3. Cancellation

You may cancel at any time; there are no contracts or early-exit fees. Your phone number remains yours — if you cancel, we simply stop answering it.

4. Acceptable use

You agree not to use Navain AI for unlawful purposes, not to attempt to disrupt or gain unauthorized access to the service, and not to misrepresent your business to callers. We may suspend service for conduct that harms the service or other customers.

5. What the AI is — and isn't

Navain AI is an automated receptionist. It can misunderstand, just like any assistant, and call summaries are provided for convenience. You remain responsible for reviewing bookings, verifying details that matter to your business, and maintaining any urgent or emergency arrangements your industry requires.

6. Availability

We target continuous 24/7 availability but do not guarantee uninterrupted service, except where a service-level agreement applies (Enterprise plans).
