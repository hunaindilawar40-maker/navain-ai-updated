How it works

From the first ring to a booked job — in four moves.

Navain AI answers on your existing business number, works like your front desk, and turns calls into calendar events and tidy summaries. Here's the whole journey, end to end.

01 · Ring

Two rings, any hour

Your caller dials the number they already know. Navain answers within two rings — at noon, at midnight, on a holiday. No hold music, no phone tree, no "please leave a message."

02 · Understand

Your greeting, your script

The agent uses the greeting and custom script you approve. It knows your services, your hours, and your service area, so callers get real answers — not canned ones.

03 · Resolve

Booked on the spot

The agent reads your live calendar, offers real slots, and confirms details. If a caller asks for a person, remains confused, or raises an urgent issue, it follows your configured handoff rules; if no one can take the call, it captures a message for follow-up.

04 · Notify

You know immediately

The moment the call ends, you get a summary by text and email — who called, what they needed, what was booked. On Growth, the caller also gets an SMS follow-up.

At the end of each week

Review call volume and where Navain helped.

At signup, share your expected weekly call volume and choose a plan. Essential and Growth fees are fixed as listed; your included-credit allowance is based on that weekly volume and confirmed before signup; there is not one published allowance that applies to every business. Enterprise is custom-priced.

A first-week usage check

After the first week, we compare actual call volume with your selected plan. If it fits, nothing changes. If it exceeds the included credits, we ask whether you want to add credits; any revised fee needs your approval before we adjust your plan.

A weekly owner summary

At the end of each week, the owner receives a summary of call volume and activity showing where Navain helped, so they can review what happened.
