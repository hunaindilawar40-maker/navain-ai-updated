A possible workflow

From a caller's question to your next step.

Your script and escalation rules determine the exact conversation. Here's the shape of a typical service request.

01 · Answer

Welcome the caller

Navain answers on your existing business number, day or night, using the greeting and voice selected for your business.

02 · Understand

Ask the right questions

Collect the issue, location, and contact details your team has approved for an initial service request.

03 · Decide

Book or hand off

Use the connected calendar when appropriate, or follow your instructions for a person, urgent issue, or callback.

04 · Follow up

Send your team a summary

Get the caller's request and agreed next step by email and SMS after the call.

Navain is not an emergency-response service. For a life-threatening emergency, callers should contact local emergency services.

Common questions

Plumbing teams, answered.

Does Navain use our existing plumbing business number?

Yes. Navain is set up to answer on your existing business number; there's no new number or hardware to install.

Can it book a service appointment?

Appointment booking is available when your calendar is connected. During setup, you choose the availability and booking rules the assistant should follow.

What if the caller needs a plumber urgently?

Set the urgent-call questions, contact, and fallback instructions during setup. Live transfer is available when configured; otherwise, Navain can capture the details for prompt follow-up. It is not an emergency-response service.

What will my team receive after a call?

Navain sends a call summary by email and SMS. The summary and any follow-up steps depend on the workflow you configure.
