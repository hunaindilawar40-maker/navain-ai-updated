Call examples

Sample workflows for a busy workday.

Every business has calls that arrive at the wrong moment. These examples show how a receptionist configured around your services, calendar, and handoff rules could help.

These are examples, not testimonials. The scenarios below are hypothetical workflows—not customer reviews, measured results, or a promise that every call will follow the same path. The agent's behavior depends on the business's approved script, connected tools, and escalation settings.
Scenario · Salon

When every chair is busy

A caller asks about an appointment while the team is with clients. With the salon's calendar connected, Navain can offer available times, book according to the salon's rules, and send a call summary.

Scenario · HVAC

A service request after hours

A caller reports that a heating or cooling system needs attention. Navain can collect the issue, location, and preferred next step, then follow the business's configured urgent-call and handoff rules.

Scenario · Dental office

A routine appointment request

A caller wants to schedule or move a visit. Navain can help with the scheduling workflow set by the practice and pass clinical questions or other exceptions to the team's chosen contact.

Scenario · Plumbing

A leak while the crew is on a job

A caller needs help while the plumber cannot pick up. Navain can capture the service address, a short description, and the caller's preferred next step, then send the team a summary.

Scenario · Gym

A question outside front-desk hours

A prospective member calls about hours, a trial, or a class. Navain can answer approved routine questions and direct requests it cannot resolve to the gym's chosen follow-up process.

Scenario · Law office

An initial request for a callback

A prospective client calls while the team is in a meeting. Navain can collect the contact details and reason for the call, then route the message for review—without giving legal advice.

Scenario · Roofing

A job request while the crew is on site

A caller asks about a roof inspection or repair. Navain can record the property location and request details, then follow the urgency and callback instructions the business has configured.

Scenario · Restaurant

A call during the dinner rush

A caller asks about hours or a reservation. Navain can provide the information the restaurant has approved and capture requests for booking or staff follow-up according to its setup.

Scenario · Accounting

A routine question during tax season

A caller asks about availability or how to reach the office. Navain can share approved general information and pass client-specific or sensitive questions to the accounting team.
