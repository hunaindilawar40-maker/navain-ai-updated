A possible workflow

From a caller's question to your next step.

Your script and escalation rules determine the exact conversation. Here's the shape of a typical service request.

01 · Answer

Welcome the caller

Navain answers on your existing office number, day or night, using the greeting selected for your office.

02 · Understand

Ask the right questions

Collect the contact details and general reason for the call that your office has approved.

03 · Decide

Schedule or hand off

Use connected scheduling when appropriate, or follow your instructions for a callback or a specific team member.

04 · Follow up

Send your team a summary

Get the caller's request and agreed next step by email and SMS after the call.

Navain is not an emergency-response service. For a life-threatening emergency, callers should contact local emergency services.

Common questions

Law offices, answered.

Does Navain use our existing office number?

Yes. Navain is set up to answer on your existing office number; there's no new number or hardware to install.

Can it schedule a consultation?

Consultation scheduling is available when your calendar is connected. During setup, you choose the availability and booking rules the assistant should follow.

Will it give callers legal advice?

No. Navain collects the details you approve and routes the request to your team. It does not give legal advice, evaluate a matter, or promise that your office will take a case.

What will my team receive after a call?

Navain sends a call summary by email and SMS. The summary and any follow-up steps depend on the workflow you configure.
