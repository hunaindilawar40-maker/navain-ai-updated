Privacy Policy

We keep this short and plain: what we collect, why we collect it, and the choices you have.

Last updated: October 5, 2026

Who we are

Navain AI (a Pontis Construction company) operates this website at navainai.com. You can reach us any time at info@navainai.com or +1 (251) 203-1002.

What we collect

  • Contact form. If you use the contact form, we receive the details you submit — name, business name, email, phone, daily call volume, and your message. The form is processed by Formspree, our form provider, and delivered to our team by email.
  • Chat widget. Messages you send to Stacy, our assistant, are sent to our server and processed by our AI provider (Groq) to generate a reply. The conversation is kept in your browser for the session; we keep only what our hosting logs collect.
  • Cookie / banner choice. When you accept or decline the cookie notice, we store that single choice in your browser's localStorage so we don't ask again. That is the only thing it stores.
  • Standard server logs. Our hosting provider (Vercel) records basic technical data such as IP address, browser type, and pages requested, for security and operations.

How we use it

  • To reply to your inquiry and prepare a demo or setup for your business.
  • To operate the chat assistant and the website itself.
  • To keep the service secure and troubleshoot problems.

We do not sell your personal data, and we do not run advertising trackers on this site.

Who we share it with

  • Formspree — processes contact form submissions on our behalf.
  • Groq — powers the chat assistant's replies.
  • Vercel — hosts the site and its serverless functions.

We only share what is needed for these providers to do their jobs, and only for the purposes above.

How long we keep it

Contact form submissions are kept as long as needed to respond and to maintain our business relationship, after which they are deleted. Chat conversations live in your browser session. Server logs are kept for a short operational window by our hosting provider.

Your choices

  • Decline the cookie notice — the site works the same either way; the banner simply won't re-appear after your choice.
  • Clear your browser's localStorage at any time to reset that choice.
  • Email info@navainai.com to ask what we hold about you or to have it deleted.

Children

This site and service are intended for businesses, not children, and we do not knowingly collect data from children under 13.

Changes

If we change this policy, we'll update this page and the date above.
