What data flows where

Written summaries — not voice demos.

Navain's connections work from the written record of a call: the summary, booking details, and follow-up text. This website never plays, publishes, or clones any real person's voice, and no integration listed here is a voice-cloning or voice-biometrics tool.

01 · Call

A call is handled

Navain answers with your approved greeting and script, and follows your booking and handoff rules.

02 · Summary

A written summary is created

Who called, what they needed, what was booked or captured, and the next step — in text.

03 · Connect

Your tools receive the text

Calendar events, summaries, follow-up texts, and CRM records — only for the connections on your plan and configured in your setup.
