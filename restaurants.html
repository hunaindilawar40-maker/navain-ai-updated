A possible workflow

From a caller's question to your next step.

Your script and escalation rules determine the exact conversation. Here's the shape of a typical service request.

01 · Answer

Welcome the caller

Navain answers on your existing restaurant number, day or night, using the greeting selected for your business.

02 · Understand

Ask the right questions

Share approved information or collect the reservation details your team has approved.

03 · Decide

Reserve or hand off

Follow your configured reservation rules when appropriate, or capture the request for staff follow-up.

04 · Follow up

Send your team a summary

Get the caller's request and agreed next step by email and SMS after the call.

Navain is not an emergency-response service. For a life-threatening emergency, callers should contact local emergency services.

Common questions

Restaurants, answered.

Does Navain use our existing restaurant number?

Yes. Navain is set up to answer on your existing restaurant number; there's no new number or hardware to install.

Can it take a reservation?

Reservation handling depends on the setup you configure. Navain can capture the party size, preferred time, and contact details, and follow your reservation rules or pass the request to your team.

What if a caller asks something outside our setup?

Set the contact and fallback instructions during setup. Navain can capture the request for staff follow-up rather than guessing.

What will my team receive after a call?

Navain sends a call summary by email and SMS. The summary and any follow-up steps depend on the workflow you configure.
