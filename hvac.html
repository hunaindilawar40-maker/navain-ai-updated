Common questions

HVAC teams, answered.

Will we need a different phone number?

No. Navain is designed to answer on your existing business number, without new phone hardware.

Can Navain diagnose a heating or cooling problem?

No. It can follow the questions and response rules you approve, collect a caller's description, and route the request for a qualified person to review.

Can it schedule service appointments?

Calendar booking is available when your live calendar is connected. You choose the availability and scheduling instructions used during setup.

How are urgent calls handled?

You set the escalation contact and fallback instructions during onboarding. Live transfer depends on the routing configured for your account; otherwise, the caller's request can be captured for follow-up.
