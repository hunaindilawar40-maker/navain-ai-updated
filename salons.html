Common questions

Salons, answered.

Will clients call the same number?

Yes. Navain is set up to answer your existing salon business number; you don't need new phone hardware.

Can it book specific services and team members?

It can follow the appointment types, availability, and booking rules configured during setup. The supported details depend on the calendar connection and your chosen workflow.

Can it answer questions about prices?

You can provide approved service information and instructions for how Navain should respond. Questions that need a personal recommendation or an answer outside that information can be handed to your team.

What if a caller wants to speak with a person?

Set your preferred human contact and fallback instructions during onboarding. Live transfer is available when configured; otherwise, Navain can take a message for follow-up.
